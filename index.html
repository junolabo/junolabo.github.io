Why these projects exist

The goal is to run a store with as little human involvement as possible. Two conditions followed from that goal.

First, irregular situations, and situations that used to need a person (a manager sent to the store, or an operator watching remotely), have to be handled by the robot as far as a robot can. Second, the store has to respond as flexibly as a staffed store.

The scope then grew to delivery and cleaning inside the company building. Every project below was added or improved for these needs. They are layers of one system, not separate projects.
